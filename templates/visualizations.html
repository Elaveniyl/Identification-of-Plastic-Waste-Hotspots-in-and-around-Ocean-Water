Ocean Garbage Patch: Pacific Drift Modeling
Tableau Workbook: OceanGarbagePatch/Pacificdrift
Open on Tableau Public

This visualization examines Lagrangian hydrodynamic particle simulations tracking surface debris transport from coastal river discharge sources into the North Pacific Subtropical Convergence Zone.

Global Ocean Plastic Data
Tableau Workbook: GlobalPlasticData/Globaldata
Open on Tableau Public

Comparative global overview highlighting macro-debris versus micro-debris density distributions across the five primary oceanic gyres (North Pacific, South Pacific, North Atlantic, South Atlantic, and Indian Ocean).

Pacific Garbage Patch Historical Survey Records
Tableau Workbook: PacificGarbagePatch/HistoryData
Open on Tableau Public

Longitudinal analysis of net trawl expeditions between 1972 and 2015, tracing the exponential growth of microplastic particle counts per square kilometer across expedition tranches.
