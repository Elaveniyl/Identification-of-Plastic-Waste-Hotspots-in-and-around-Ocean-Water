Academic Research & Methodology

About Stop Marine Pollution

A computational research project exploring artificial intelligence, spatial GIS modeling, and formal automata theory to detect, prioritize, and manage oceanic plastic waste accumulation.

Problem Statement

The Oceanic Plastic Crisis

Over 5.25 trillion plastic particles currently float on the ocean surface, with approximately 8 million metric tons entering marine environments each year. In oceanic gyres, plastic debris breaks down under ultraviolet radiation into toxic microplastics that enter the global food web.

Traditional cleanup operations face major operational constraints: searching vast open ocean expanses without targeted intelligence is economically and energetically prohibitive.

Our initiative addresses this challenge by coupling automated debris classification with quantitative geographic hotspot prioritization, enabling intervention teams to focus resources on the highest mass-density zones.

Surface plastic fragments in oceanic convergence zones resist biodegradation and accumulate persistent organic pollutants (POPs).
Methodology

Integrated Technical Framework

Computer Vision Pipeline

Preprocesses RGB imagery to $299 \times 299$ resolution and feeds normalized tensors into an Inception-v3 deep convolutional network trained across six maritime debris categories.

  • Input shape: (1, 299, 299, 3)
  • Preprocessing: Normalized to [-1.0, 1.0]
  • Output: Categorical label prediction
Spatial GIS Prioritisation

Applies a multi-criteria Hotspot Prioritization Index (HPI) to 3,532 standardized field observations from Laurent Lebreton et al. (2018), ranking points into Critical, Moderate, and Baseline tiers.

  • Weighting: Mass (50%), Gyre (35%), Year (15%)
  • Coordinate System: WGS 84 (EPSG:4326)
  • Clustering: Multi-point geospatial bounds
Formal Automata Modeling

Formalizes the lifecycle of an environmental debris observation through an 8-state Deterministic Finite Automaton (DFA) $M = (Q, \Sigma, \delta, q_0, F)$ enforcing mathematical transition invariants.

  • States: $Q = 8$, Alphabet: $\Sigma = 7$
  • Initial: $q_0 = \text{DRAFT}$
  • Accepting: $F = \{\text{ARCHIVED, REJECTED}\}$
