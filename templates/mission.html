Our Research Mission

Targeting marine plastic pollution mitigation through the rigorous synthesis of deep learning computer vision, spatial geostatistics, and formal state automata.

Mitigating Ocean Pollution with Quantitative AI

70% of the oxygen we breathe and 97% of the world’s water supply is provided by oceans. Over the past decade, global plastic production has exceeded the total output of the entire preceding century. Because plastics fragment under solar ultraviolet radiation rather than mineralizing, durable microplastics persist across centuries.

By modeling ocean plastic hotspots with quantitative precision, this project delivers actionable intelligence for marine cleanup vessels, oceanographic researchers, and environmental policy stakeholders.
