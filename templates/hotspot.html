Hotspot Prioritization Index Formula

The Hotspot Prioritization Index (HPI) is a standardized multi-criteria score bounded to $[0, 100]$:

$$\text{HPI} = 0.50 \cdot C_{\text{norm}} + 0.35 \cdot I_{\text{GPGP}} + 0.15 \cdot Y_{\text{norm}}$$
  • • $C_{\text{norm}} \in [0, 100]$: Log-transformed, normalized surface plastic mass concentration.
  • • $I_{\text{GPGP}} \in \{0, 100\}$: Binary indicator for containment within the subtropical gyre boundary ($0.1\text{ g/km}^2$ boundary).
  • • $Y_{\text{norm}} \in [0, 100]$: Normalized temporal recency weighting modern survey expeditions.
Dataset Provenance & Scientific Notice

Data Source: Laurent Lebreton et al. (2018), "Evidence that the Great Pacific Garbage Patch is rapidly accumulating plastic", Nature Scientific Reports 8:4666. Dataset aggregated from 655 surface net tows by The Ocean Cleanup Mega Expedition and 2,877 historical trawls (Scripps Institution of Oceanography, Algalita Marine Research, SEAPLEX).

Notice: Observations represent physical marine survey expeditions (1972–2015). They serve as a calibrated scientific baseline for spatial prioritization models and are not real-time telemetry.
